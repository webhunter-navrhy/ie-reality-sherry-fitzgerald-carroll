About the property

Description property

Sherry FitzGerald Carroll are delighted to bring No 51 Marlmount Park to the market, a stunningly presented four bedroom semi detached family home offering a wealth of fine finishes and features.

Marlmount is an exceptional development of exclusive family homes located in a privileged setting to the south of Dundalk on the Old Dublin Road, a short distance from the seaside village of Blackrock, Dundalk town centre and with easy access to the M1 Motorway.

A welcoming four bedroom semi detached 'Hazel' style family home, extending to c.1,292 sq ft offering generous and light filled living including, entrance hall, sitting room, kitchen/dining room, utility and WC at ground floor. Upstairs there are four generous bedrooms with master en-suite and family bathroom.

A turn key home filled with quality fittings that is sure to appeal to a wide range of discerning purchasers, viewing is highly recommended.
